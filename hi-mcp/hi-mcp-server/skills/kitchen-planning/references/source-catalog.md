# Kitchen Planning Source Catalog

Optional research reference. Load only to verify a source-backed claim, answer a provenance/citation question, or update the guidance. Routine planning does not require this file; the rules, classifications, and operational qualifications are in the skill and [planning reference](./planning-reference.md).

Research checked: 2026-10-09. Scope: residential kitchens, Germany/EU default. The sources below were retrieved for the stated topics. The full DIN standard, paid NKBA handbook, and project-specific appliance installation manuals were not examined. The retrieval date does not establish technical currency; verify current applicable requirements and exact product instructions for each real project.

## Sources

### S1. AMK: Planen und gestalten

https://www.amk-ratgeber-kueche.de/kueche/planen-gestalten

German kitchen-industry association guidance. Supports functional zones, point-of-use storage, approximately 150 mm elbow-to-worktop difference, individualized working heights, full-extension drawers, early service planning, lighting and accessibility considerations. This is consumer guidance, not a regulation or a complete technical standard. Do not adopt every safety/marketing statement literally; see the evidence boundaries in the planning reference.

### S2. IKEA Germany: Gestaltungsideen fuer reibungslose Ablaeufe

https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/kitchen/how-to/kueche-effizient-planen-pub6d21e890/

Supports generous preparation space between sink and hob, proximity of waste and dishwasher, and placing cookware/tools at their point of use. Uses the work triangle as a general planning aid. Does not establish a statutory sink-to-hob distance.

### S3. Blum: Speisenvorbereitung leicht gemacht

https://www.blum-inspirations.com/de-eu/wissen/speisenvorbereitung-leicht-gemacht-der-passende-schrank-unterstuetzt-sie-dabei

Manufacturer ergonomic guidance, article dated March 2022. Recommends a preparation area between sink and hob, approximately 900 mm wide, and advises at least 800 mm; preparation tools and bowls belong nearby in organized drawers. These dimensions are ergonomic recommendations, not proof of product clearance or German code compliance.

### S4. Blum: Geschirr perfekt organisiert

https://www.blum-inspirations.com/de-eu/wissen/geschirr-perfekt-organisiert-im-unterschrank-mit-auszuegen

Manufacturer guidance, article dated June 2022. Supports dining cutlery and dishes together in full-extension base storage, nearby cups/glasses, and proximity to the dishwasher to reduce unloading travel. It does not supply a universal maximum distance or guarantee collision-free access.

### S5. Blum: Die ergonomische Kueche

https://www.blum-inspirations.com/de-eu/wissen/die-ergonomische-kueche-das-muss-man-beachten

Manufacturer guidance, article dated July 2021. Explains access benefits of full-extension base drawers and overhead lift fronts versus projecting doors. Benefits still depend on actual reach, opening height, operating force and room geometry; powered fittings are optional, not necessary for every accessible kitchen.

### S6. AMK: Geraetetechnik

https://www.amk-ratgeber-kueche.de/kueche/geraetetechnik

Supports early extraction planning, the importance of capture efficiency, storage consumed by downdraft equipment, and appliance feature/energy/noise considerations. Room-volume airflow rules of thumb alone do not constitute a ventilation design. The combustion-safety checklist in the planning reference is a professional-review trigger, not a quoted clearance rule from this page.

### S7. nullbarriere.de: DIN 18040-2 Kuechen, Essplatz

https://nullbarriere.de/din18040-2-kuechen.htm

Specialist secondary summary of German dwelling accessibility guidance. Describes 1200/1500 movement-space dimensions and wheelchair considerations. It is not the official DIN text. Verify the current standard, project category, legal/contractual applicability and complete requirements before claiming conformity.

### S8. IKEA Germany: Kuechenplanung

https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/kitchen/kuechenplanung-pub6003162f/

Supports surveying in mm, marking openings/services, and staged planning, checking, ordering and installation. Links to current system-specific planning and installation documents. Those documents and the chosen product manuals must be obtained for a real order.

### S9. IKEA Germany: Schubladen organisieren

https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/kitchen/how-to/kuechenschraenke-und-schubladen-organisieren-8-ideen-die-zeit-und-platz-sparen-pube8ed7a50/

Supports full-extension storage, functional grouping, protected knives, organizers and reachable everyday contents. Named fittings are examples, not a recommendation to adopt one vendor.

### S10. NKBA Planning Guidelines: Regional Boundary

https://nkba.org/planning-guidelines/

The public overview describes guidance intended for US/Canadian design practice and its code context. Only the overview was consulted, not the paid handbook. Do not label NKBA dimensions as German/EU requirements or cite handbook clauses not actually verified.
