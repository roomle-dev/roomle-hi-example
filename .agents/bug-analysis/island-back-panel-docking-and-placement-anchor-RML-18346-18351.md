# Island back panel protrudes and island lands off: the docking derivation and the placement anchor are the root cause (RML-18346, RML-18351)

> **Type:** Bug analysis
>
> **Status:** Open — the fix is implemented in roomle-ui `feat/hi-planning-assistant` (decisions D132–D135: row/corner-aware `behind` anchoring, order-independent placement anchor over the whole group, module-carried catalog dimensions with `cornerBackLengthsMm`, named uncarried root overrides), pending the model verification of the tickets' acceptance criteria on bo-test
>
> **Date:** 2026-11-01
>
> **Tickets:** [RML-18346](https://roomle.atlassian.net/browse/RML-18346) (comment 156767), [RML-18351](https://roomle.atlassian.net/browse/RML-18351) (comment 156766)
>
> **Base:** roomle-ui `release/bo-test` `9b8181406` — the planner the probes ran against (`https://www.roomle.com/t/bo-test/`, the same build the tickets' 2026-10-31 snapshots were planned with; the local `feat/hi-planning-assistant` `c7aeb092d` carries the same HI tool code); roomle-hi-example `fix/back-panel-anchor-island-first-placement-RML-18346-18351` `cc8e35b8`

Supersedes the instruction-level reading of [kitchen-back-panel-anchor-and-island-first-placement-RML-18346-18351.md](kitchen-back-panel-anchor-and-island-first-placement-RML-18346-18351.md): the fixes proposed there were implemented and reverted — they treated symptoms. The probes below show the defects are in the tool layer: the docking the server derives from `behind`, and the placement anchor the server picks.

**The ticket owner's suspicion is confirmed by both the decoded incident snapshots and model-free probes:** the tools lack a feature to close the back of a free-standing row whose back side is made of several modules — in kitchen 16 a corner module plus straight modules ([RML-18346 comment 156767](https://roomle.atlassian.net/browse/RML-18346?focusedCommentId=156767)), in kitchen 5 an island of three modules ([RML-18351 comment 156766](https://roomle.atlassian.net/browse/RML-18351?focusedCommentId=156766)). `behind` anchors one panel at one named root's `BackBottom` (`hi-group-layout.ts:886-899`):
- On a **straight island row** (`RML-18351` attempt 1 `ps_tevc8l1fd4kpjvwn9uapk7iqq4ixqjh`), naming any root other than `rowIndex 1` (`d32ed7` at `rowIndex 3` in `ps_tevc8l1…`, or `u2` at `rowIndex 2` in Probe A1) starts the panel at that unit's left edge and runs it `1800 mm` toward `+x` — **protruding 600–1200 mm past the island's right end and leaving the other units uncovered**, with `corrections: null` and `hint: null`.
- On a **corner-plus-straight peninsula** (`RML-18346` attempt 1 `ps_tev8txpav3xpk8ean5fg7mnzv0s5xse`), a corner article (`UERTB90`) has `LeftBackBottom` (`661 mm`) and `RightBackBottom` (`1161 mm`), not `BackBottom`: `behind` a corner article is dropped (`hi-group-layout.ts:460-466`, `"a corner article has no back to dock behind - ignored"`) and `defaultLink` (`:607-620`) re-docks the panel as a row cabinet `rightOf` the last unit (Probe D3); `behind` a straight unit covers only the straight units (`planX = [-24, 1176]` in `ps_tev8tx…`, leaving the corner's `661 mm` back `[-685, -24]` open) or protrudes 660–1800 mm past the peninsula when given the full back length (the unstored protrusion attempt of RML-18346 and Probes D1/D2). In the final snapshot `ps_tevl1rdg5nvbmel126ta3mwrxv9440q`, the agent had to work around this by merging a **second** `RWV` (`df0871`, `656 mm`) onto the corner's `LeftBackBottom` (`mode: StartStart`).

## Root causes (file:line)

1. **There is no operation that closes the whole back side of a free-standing row — `behind` anchors one panel at one named root's back line** — `hi-group-layout.ts:460-466, 607-620, 886-899`: `behind` becomes `BackBottom → BackBottom` with mode `StartEnd`/`EndStart` at the *named neighbour's* end, so the panel starts at that root's end and runs along that root's back line. Four consequences, proven by the incident snapshots and probes:
   - **Mid-row or far-end `behind` on a straight island (`RML-18351` `ps_tevc8l1fd4kpjvwn9uapk7iqq4ixqjh` and Probe A1):** Named `behind: "d32ed7"` (`rowIndex 3` of a 3-unit island, `planX = [4000, 4600]`), an `1800 mm` `RWV` starts at `planX = 4000` and spans `planX = [4000, 5800]`, protruding `1200 mm` past the island (`[2800, 4600]`) and leaving `rowIndex 1..2` (`[2800, 4000]`) bare — with `corrections: null` and `hint: null`, even though `withRowIndices` already computed the row.
   - **Corner-plus-straight peninsula back not covered by `behind` on a straight unit (`RML-18346` `ps_tev8txpav3xpk8ean5fg7mnzv0s5xse`, unstored attempt, and Probes D1/D2):** In `ps_tev8tx…`, `RWV` (`1200 mm`) docked `behind` `66e857` (`leftOf` leg) covers `6b4b75` + `66e857` (`planX = [-24, 1176]`) and leaves corner `6ede4f`'s `661 mm` `LeftBackBottom` (`planX = [-685, -24]`) bare; if docked `behind` `6b4b75` or in a `rightOf` peninsula (`c1` → `rightOf` `u1` → `rightOf` `u2`, Probes D1/D2), `StartEnd` starts the panel after the corner (`x = 1161` or `1761`) and extends it away from the corner (`+x`), leaving `c1`'s `1161 mm` `RightBackBottom` bare and protruding 660–1800 mm past the peninsula end.
   - **`behind` a corner article is dropped and re-docked `rightOf` the row end (`hi-group-layout.ts:460-466, 607-620`, Probe D3):** Although `UERTB90` exposes `LeftBackBottom` (`661 mm`) and `RightBackBottom` (`1161 mm`), `hi-group-layout.ts:460-466` drops `behind` on any corner article (`"a corner article has no back to dock behind - ignored"`) and `defaultLink` (`:607-620`) places the `RWV` as a floor cabinet `rightOf` the last unit (`rowIndex 4`, width `4776 mm`). Yet `ps_tevl1rdg5nvbmel126ta3mwrxv9440q` proves that `LeftBackBottom -> RWV:BackBottom (StartStart)` on `UERTB90` starts `RWV` at `planX = -685` (`pos = [0, 0, 3042], rotY = 0`) and runs it in `+planX` along the exact back line (`z = 3042`) of the `leftOf` straight units (and symmetrically `RightBackBottom -> RWV:BackBottom (StartEnd)` starts `RWV` at `x = -261` and runs it in `+x` across a `rightOf` leg).
   - **Corner back lengths are hidden from the catalog (`RML-18346` `ps_tev8tx…`):** `UERTB90` serves `mod_Depth: 561, mod_Width: 900`, which does not expose the `661 mm` (`LeftBackBottom`) or `1161 mm` (`RightBackBottom`) corner back lengths along a peninsula leg, so the agent either sizes the panel to the straight units only (`1200 mm` in `ps_tev8tx…`, leaving `661 mm` open) or adds `mod_Width = 900` (`2100 mm`, protruding `239 mm`).
2. **The placement anchor and reported `pos` ignore the rear panel, depend on root order, and the island is placed in the same first call before the wall kitchen's footprint is known (`RML-18351` `ps_tevc8l1…` → `ps_teu5dw6irh9fmj3w2a8k0tsd4xmnf5h`)** — `hi-group-placement.ts:89` (`findAnchorRoot`: `rootsById.get(startRootId) ?? roots[0]`, then walks carriers/`leftOf`), used by `toRepositioningData` (`:257-`), `groupPlacementByPoint` (`:337-`), and `groupAnchorFrame` (`:315-`) which produces the reported `position.pos` via `positionInPlacementFrame` (`:361-`):
   - **The `250 mm` Y offset in `RML-18351` (`pos.y = 1450 → 1200`) equals the `RWV` panel depth:** In `ps_tevc8l1…` and `ps_tev11tay…` (where `a6b788` `UTB60` is `roots[0]`), `posGroup` and the reported `pos = [2800, 1450, 0]` anchor on `a6b788`'s `BackBottom` (`planY = 1450`), while the `RWV` panel extends `220 mm` **past** `pos` on the back side (`planY = [1450, 1670]`, Probe B2). Because the served text describes `posGroup` / `pos` as *"the group's back left bottom corner"*, placing `posGroup[1] = 1450` to align the island's back with the end of the left-wall leg (`planY = 1426`) actually pushes the panel to `1670`; in `ps_teu5dw…`, the agent had to subtract `250 mm` (`posGroup[1] = 1200`) so the panel's outer back landed at `planY = 1420`.
   - **Root list order flips the anchor (Probe A3):** If `RWV` is listed first in `roots`, `findAnchorRoot` picks `RWV` (`rotY = 180`), flipping the group's footprint Y range from `[-617, 220]` (A1) to `[-220, 617]` (A3) for the exact same `posGroup`/`posRotationZ`.
   - **First-call X offset (`pos.x = 2800 → 1100`, `ΔX = -1700 mm`):** Creating the corner-placed L-kitchen (`planX = [-685, 2376]`) and the point-placed island in the same first `create-or-replace-groups` call forces the agent to guess `posGroup` before seeing the L-kitchen's placed coordinates, and to confuse the right end (`2800` vs `2900`, matching its choice of `d32ed7` at `rowIndex 3`) with the left-back placement corner (`1100`).
3. **The catalog serves a calculated template dimension (`mod_Width: 1000`) that `RWV` does not carry, and `checkRootOverrides` silently drops uncarried root overrides** — `hi-plan-context.ts:663-665` (`compactArticle` takes every `Dim` of the template without checking `assignedAttributes`) and `hi-tool-attributes.ts:480-496` + `hi-attribute-check.ts:61-62` (`collectAllowedSubModuleAttributes` inspects sub-modules only). In the five 2026-10-31 snapshots the applied library description (`RWV` / `mod_IslandBackwallWidth`) already steered the agent to `mod_IslandBackwallWidth`, so this was not the primary trigger there, but `compactArticle` still serves `mod_Width = 1000` on `RWV` and `checkRootOverrides` still gives no feedback if `mod_Width` is sent on `RWV`.

Why the current code produces the wrong results: all three are server-side derivation gaps — the server derives the docking from a single named relation and the placement anchor from a single cabinet/list-order root instead of from the row/group geometry it already computes, and it serves template dimensions instead of the module's. The library calculates exactly what it is told; the wrong instructions reach it.

## Proposed clean fix (roomle-ui, server-side derivation — no guard, no instruction-only workaround)

1. **Give the tools a real back-closing derivation across straight and corner-led rows.** In `relationsToDocking` (`hi-group-layout.ts`):
   - When a row-less unit (such as `RWV`) docks `behind` any root of a floor row (or `behind` the corner article that starts a peninsula leg), walk the connected row (`withRowIndices` plus any adjacent corner at the leg's start) to determine the **full exposed back side** and anchor the panel at the **row's start end**:
     - **Straight row (e.g. `RML-18351`):** anchor on `rowIndex 1`'s `BackBottom` (`StartEnd`), even if the agent wrote `behind` a middle or right-end root (`d32ed7` at `rowIndex 3`).
     - **Corner + `leftOf` leg (e.g. `RML-18346`, `6ede4f` `UERTB90` → `leftOf` `6b4b75` → `leftOf` `66e857`):** when the panel covers the corner's back too (or is docked `behind` the corner or straight leg), anchor on the corner's `LeftBackBottom -> BackBottom (StartStart)` so one `RWV` starts at the corner's outer edge (`planX = -685`) and runs continuously across the corner (`661 mm`) and straight units (`1200 mm`, total `1861 mm`).
     - **Corner + `rightOf` leg (Probes D1–D3, `c1` `UERTB90` → `rightOf` `u1` → `rightOf` `u2`):** anchor on the corner's `RightBackBottom -> BackBottom (StartEnd)` so one `RWV` starts at `RightBackBottom.start` and runs continuously across the corner (`1161 mm`) and straight units.
     - **Never silently drop `behind` on a corner article into `defaultLink` (`rightOf` the row end):** only drop `behind` when the corner has no free back vector on that leg.
   - If the panel's authored length (`mod_IslandBackwallWidth` / `mod_Width`) differs from the connected row's back length (e.g. `1200 mm` for straight units only when the corner adds `661 mm`, or a protruding width), either auto-adjust / report the exact full back length (`1861 mm`, listing `661 mm` corner back + `1200 mm` straight units) in `corrections`. Tests in `hi-group-layout-test.ts` and `create-or-replace-groups-placement-test.ts`.
2. **Make the placement anchor geometric and order-independent.** In `findAnchorRoot` / `groupAnchorFrame` / `positionInPlacementFrame` (`hi-group-placement.ts`): never pick a `behind`-docked panel as `findAnchorRoot`, and anchor `posGroup` and the reported `position.pos` at the **back-left bottom corner of the whole calculated group in the placement frame** (including the rear panel's depth when present), independent of `roots` array order. Then `posGroup` and `position.pos` mean the exact "group's back left bottom corner" described in the served text (`A1 == A3`, and `B2` no longer extends `220 mm` past `pos`). In addition, guide the agent (`hi-mcp-server.ts` / `image-planning/SKILL.md`) to place wall/corner groups first (or compute `posGroup` from their returned `footprint`) before point-placing a freestanding island. Tests in `placement-frame-test.ts`, `hi-group-placement-test.ts`, `agent-frame-test.ts`.
3. **Serve the module's dimensions and report uncarried root overrides** — in `compactArticle` (`hi-plan-context.ts:663`), filter template `Dim` attributes by the root module's `assignedAttributes` (or include the module's `Dim` attribute such as `mod_IslandBackwallWidth`), and in `checkRootOverrides` (`hi-tool-attributes.ts:480`), report overrides that neither the root's `assignedAttributes` nor any sub-module carries, naming the root's actual size attribute. Tests in `hi-plan-context-test.ts` and `hi-tool-attributes-test.ts`.
