Affected repositories:

- **roomle-hi-example:** the reports of the Azure series, this unnumbered comparison and the indexes.
- **roomle-ui:** the planner measured per report; in 06 proposal A, `get-skill` serving a room skill with its planning reference.

This is the single unnumbered comparison of the Azure series. The OpenAI series 01–05
([its comparison](iklw-oa-rc-cmp-2026-10-26.md)) is the same model on direct OpenAI; its turns are
compared with this series for the shape of a turn — the calls and what each one does —, never for
the seconds. The baseline of the seconds on this deployment is the test session of 2026-10-27, one
run per test on the planner of bo-test, so a difference beyond the spread of two repetitions is a
finding, a smaller one is not.

## Decision

**Proposal A is accepted.** Every run of 06 creates at call 2 and none fetches the planning reference apart; the image kitchen comes 6.7 s earlier than the session's run on this deployment, the text kitchen 1.4 s earlier in the mean of two runs, within their spread (28.3–32.3 s, of which the planner's create tool took 3.3 and 8.1 s); the reasoning of the reference round shows up in the create calls (219 / 330 and 319 / 332 reasoning tokens). The kitchens are those of benchmarks 04 and 05 and of the test's expectation. roomle-ui e8964c3df on `feat/hi-mcp-performance` stays. What remains before the first attempt is the create call itself — 18–25 s in an image creation, 21 s in a text creation, more than half of it the wait for its first output —, which proposal C addresses, and the two rounds that G would fold into one.
