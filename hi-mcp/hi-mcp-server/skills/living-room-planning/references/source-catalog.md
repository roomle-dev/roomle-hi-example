# Living Room Planning Source Catalog

Optional research/provenance file. Do not load for routine planning or solely to expand a source ID. Load when the user asks for citations, a claim needs verification, sources conflict, or the skill is being maintained.

Research reviewed: 2026-10-09. Scope: generic domestic living-room planning with Germany/EU defaults. Summaries are original planning notes, not reproduced articles. The operational rules and qualifications are in [the planning reference](./planning-reference.md); the workflow is in [the skill](../SKILL.md).

## S1 - IKEA Germany: activity zones and reset storage

- Source: [So gestaltest du drei Arbeitsbereiche in einem Raum](https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/living-room/how-to/wohnzimmer-aufteilen-pubd020bb20/).
- Type: retailer guidance; German market.
- Retrieved support: separate work/relaxation areas, visual screening, movable storage, keeping work items together for easy reset.
- Applied: activity and simultaneous-use brief, nearby storage, explicit setup/reset actions.
- Limits: product inspiration, not dimensional or acoustic compliance evidence. Do not generalize the sofa/laptop examples into suitable full-time workstation ergonomics. Screens do not establish speech privacy or sound isolation.

## S2 - IKEA Germany: living-room lighting

- Source: [Bestmoegliche Nutzung deiner Wohnzimmerbeleuchtung](https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/living-room/how-to/wohnzimmerbeleuchtung-ideen-pub75352e80/).
- Type: retailer guidance; German market.
- Retrieved support: combine general, task, and mood lighting; support concentrated tasks with appropriate surrounding light; cabinet lighting and adjustable controls.
- Applied: activity-specific lighting, contrast/glare review, multiple controllable sources.
- Limits: does not establish mandatory domestic illuminance, colour temperature, or a required number of lamps. Battery-powered lighting still needs energy and charging; the page's casual wording is not an electrical claim.

## S3 - IKEA Germany: TV furniture

- Source: [So waehlst du eine TV-Bank, die bestens in dein Zuhause passt](https://www.ikea.com/de/de/rooms/living-room/how-to/stilvolle-tv-baenke-pub8de44a60/).
- Type: retailer guidance; German market.
- Retrieved support: screen centre at seated eye height; open/closed storage choices; cable openings; suggests cabinet wider than screen.
- Applied: seated-eye planning, storage configuration, cable access.
- Limits: furniture wider than the screen does not establish stability. Check feet, depth, loads, anchors, and exact manuals independently. Product range dimensions/prices are not universal design rules and are not embedded in the skill.

## S4 - Sony: wall-mounted TV height

- Source: [What is the recommended height when mounting the TV on a wall?](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00026357).
- Type: manufacturer general support; US site. Retrieved page displayed article ID 00026358 and modification date 2019-08-19 despite the requested URL.
- Retrieved support: height depends on preference and seating; generally centre the screen at viewers' eye level; model-specific off-axis image characteristics need checking.
- Applied: actual seated eye measurements, posture trials, per-seat sightline checks.
- Limits: not a fixed floor-height rule, comfort guarantee, bracket specification, or installation manual. Panel viewing-angle specifications differ from the visual angle a screen occupies.

## S5 - Sony: viewing distance qualifications

- Source: [Recommended viewing distance for watching TV](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/articles/00008601).
- Type: manufacturer general support; US site. Retrieved page displayed article ID 00008602 and modification date 2025-01-17.
- Retrieved support: distance depends on screen size/resolution, content, surroundings, and the viewer; adapt reference distances to preference and room layout.
- Applied: avoid a universal screen-diagonal multiplier; trial the intended screen and content.
- Limits: the fetched text did not expose the numeric content inside the 4K/HD/SD sections. No numerical distance recommendation is attributed to this source. The reference's screen-angle example is elementary geometry, not a Sony recommendation.

## S6 - nullbarriere: living/sleeping-room accessibility

- Source: [DIN 18040-2 Wohnraum, Schlafraum](https://nullbarriere.de/din18040-2-wohnraum-schlafraum.htm).
- Context: [DIN 18040-2 index](https://nullbarriere.de/din18040-2.htm), identifying edition 2011-09 and distinguishing ordinary barrier-free use from unrestricted wheelchair use (R).
- Type: German specialist secondary standard summary, not the normative text.
- Retrieved support: turning/movement areas of 1200 x 1200 mm and 1500 x 1500 mm for wheelchair use; depths along/in front of furniture of 900 mm and 1500 mm respectively; flexibility and functional access matter.
- Applied: qualified accessibility prompts and provisional checks; do not import ordinary coffee-table gaps into accessible approaches.
- Limits: current edition, legal adoption by federal state, contractual applicability, exact measurement provisions, and exceptions need verification from authoritative project sources. Full standard text was not reviewed. Bedroom examples and unrelated dimensions are not generalized into living-room rules.

## S7 - Verbraucherzentrale: keep radiator heat flow clear

- Source: [Heizung optimieren und Heizkosten sparen](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/energie/heizen-und-warmwasser/heizung-optimieren-und-heizkosten-sparen-30096).
- Type: German consumer advice; retrieved article dated 2026-10-08.
- Retrieved support: long curtains, furniture, and covers in front of radiators can trap heat, impair room warming, and distort thermostat sensing; keep radiators unobstructed.
- Applied: furniture/shading layout must preserve heat flow and usable controls.
- Limits: no universal clearance is inferred. Heating-system modification, fireplace clearances, and cold-wall moisture assessment require their own evidence. Unrelated legislation and numerical savings on this page are not used.

## S8 - US CPSC: furniture and TV tip-over prevention

- Source: [Anchor It!](https://www.anchorit.gov/).
- Type: US government consumer-safety campaign.
- Retrieved support: anchor susceptible furniture and televisions using suitable anchoring equipment; follow installation instructions.
- Applied: include anchoring in layout/installation planning, account for wall construction, and do not assume a low or visually stable unit needs no assessment.
- Limits: US regulations and typical wall construction are not German/EU requirements. Exact product manuals, appropriate substrate/fixings, and professional review control project installation. This skill does not reproduce a generic DIY anchoring procedure.

## S9 - US CPSC: window-covering cords

- Source: [Window Covering Cords](https://www.cpsc.gov/Safety-Education/Safety-Education-Centers/Window-Covering) (retrieved canonical destination: [Go Cordless](https://www.cpsc.gov/gocordless)).
- Type: US government consumer-safety guidance.
- Retrieved support: cordless coverings are the safest choice around young children; keep furniture that enables access away from windows/cords; mitigation of existing cords does not eliminate the hazard.
- Applied: check climbing routes and reachable cords when positioning sofas, shelves, and play areas; prefer cordless coverings.
- Limits: not a claim about current German/EU product law or a complete child-safety audit. No US retrofit dimensions or regulatory requirements are transposed into the skill.
