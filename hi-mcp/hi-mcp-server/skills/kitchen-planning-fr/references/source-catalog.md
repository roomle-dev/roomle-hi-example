# France Kitchen Planning Source Catalog

Optional research/provenance file for [the skill](../SKILL.md) and [its operational reference](planning-reference.md). Do not load during routine planning unless a claim needs verification, the user requests citations, or guidance is being updated.

Reviewed on 2026-10-09. Source claims are paraphrased. A retrieval date is not a guarantee of current legal applicability. Commercial guidance is identified as such; general manufacturer advice is not a selected product's installation manual.

## FR-S1: Legrand, NF C 15-100 professional guide

- URL: https://www.legrand.fr/pro/normes-et-reglementations/norme-nf-c-15-100/norme-nf-c-15-100-suivez-le-guide
- Publisher/type: Legrand, manufacturer technical summary; page headed "Norme NF C 15-100 en 2026 : on vous dit tout."
- Inspected: introduction, structure of the standard, circuits prises, and related scope text.
- Supports: 2024 revision and reported transition dates; new/entirely renovated installation context; ordinary kitchen socket reservations; separate specialized appliance connections; four worktop sockets within the six-socket case; optional additional hood outlet at 1800 mm minimum above finished floor.
- Limits: not the complete standard or an independent legal interpretation. The page uses `< 4 m2` and `> 4 m2` without explicitly resolving equality. Do not convert that gap into a legal rule. Its general appliance circuit examples are not a complete design for any particular supply or appliance inventory. Full placement clauses were not independently inspected.
- Use in skill: French electrical intake, reservations, electrician handoff, and boundary-case test. No DIY wiring instructions or automatic retrofit obligation.

## FR-S2: Schneider Electric, NF C 15-100 overview

- URL: https://www.se.com/fr/fr/work/support/local/reglementation/norme-nfc15-100/
- Publisher/type: Schneider Electric France, manufacturer standards overview.
- Supports: identification of the 2024 revision and availability of updated guidance; AFNOR standard versus explanatory guide distinction.
- Limits: detailed expandable answers and the downloadable guide were not inspected. Does not independently verify the numeric kitchen reservations used from FR-S1.
- Use in skill: cross-check of the revision context, not project compliance evidence.

## FR-S3: Sauter, hood versus VMC

- URL: https://www.confort-sauter.com/aide/vmc/installation/ma-hotte-de-cuisine-peut-elle-remplacer-une-vmc/
- Publisher/type: Sauter, manufacturer ventilation FAQ.
- Supports: continuous air renewal by VMC versus intermittent removal of cooking emissions by a hood; their complementary functions.
- Limits: blanket marketing statements about mandatory VMC are not used as universal retrofit law. This FAQ did not establish legal permission or a universal prohibition for powered-hood connection to a communal system.
- Use in skill: retain general ventilation, investigate the actual system, and require professional compatibility/permission evidence.

## FR-S4: Service Public, furnished-dwelling equipment

- URL: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34769
- Title: "Bail d'habitation : qu'est-ce qu'un logement meuble ?"
- Publisher/type: French public-service administrative guidance; page verification date 2026-03-06.
- Supports: minimum equipment for a furnished principal-residence lease, including bail mobilite; cooking plates, oven OR microwave, refrigerator/freezer alternative, dishes, utensils, table/seats, storage and lighting; inventory documentation.
- Limits: not a universal specification for owner occupation, location nue, holiday rentals or every overseas jurisdiction. The whole dwelling has additional requirements beyond the kitchen. Verify the current applicable legal basis and territorial scope for the project.
- Use in skill: scoped rental branch and equipment distinction, not a complete decency certificate.

## FR-S5: IKEA France, practical kitchen layout

- URL: https://www.ikea.com/fr/fr/rooms/kitchen/how-to/idees-dagencement-pratique-pour-la-cuisine-pub6d21e890/
- Title: "Idees d'agencement pratique pour la cuisine."
- Publisher/type: French-market retailer planning advice.
- Supports: preparation between sink and cooking, nearby dishwasher/waste, storage at point of use, and several layout forms.
- Limits: activity-triangle and equal-distance language is advisory, not mandatory. One retailer cannot establish the prevalence of national household habits.
- Use in skill: evidence that familiar ergonomic principles also apply in France; no invented French triangle or style requirement.

## FR-S6: Blum, preparation storage and worktop

- URL: https://www.blum-inspirations.com/de-eu/wissen/speisenvorbereitung-leicht-gemacht-der-passende-schrank-unterstuetzt-sie-dabei
- Publisher/type: Blum, German-language European manufacturer ergonomics/planning advice, reused from the general kitchen research.
- Supports: preparation worktop and related storage; approximately 900 mm recommended working width and around 800 mm lower ergonomic recommendation.
- Limits: not French regulation and not a universal minimum. Usable clear surface, tasks, products and users matter more than a nominal run length.
- Use in skill: explicitly labeled transferable ergonomic recommendation, not country-specific compliance.

- Publisher/type: DHUP-CSTB-Cerema accessibility portal, regulatory orientation.

## Research exclusions and maintenance

- Service Public's rental-decency overview at https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2042 was only partially accessible. Its metropolitan/overseas distinction informed the intake; detailed kitchen-equipment provisions were not verified.
- IZI by EDF's kitchen-electrical article was inspected but not adopted as authority because its broad claims and comments contained ambiguities or conflicts. In particular, no universal 600 mm sink/hob rule, kitchen bathroom-like volumes, mandatory kitchen RJ45, or automatic whole-dwelling upgrade on sale is adopted.
- IKEA France's hood article was inspected but is not used for generic hood heights, filter lifetimes or claims that recirculation removes humidity outdoors.
- Primary ventilation legislation could not be retrieved from Legifrance during research. No numeric statutory ventilation rate or categorical communal-duct legal rule is asserted.
- Copropriete permissions, landlord agreement, quotations and trade coordination are practical due-diligence checks. Voting thresholds, permit procedures, statutory quote requirements and VAT rates were not established and are not prescribed.
- No primary text or current project-specific French accessibility clause was verified. Do not turn portal orientation into an accessibility certificate.
- Before updating a numeric requirement, check the full current source, territorial/project applicability, exceptions and measurement definition; update the operational guidance and source qualification together.
